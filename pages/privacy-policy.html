Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shortify ("hum", "hamari service") aapki privacy ko seriously leta hai. Ye policy batati hai kaunsa data collect hota hai aur wo kaise use hota hai.

1. Kaunsa Data Collect Hota Hai

  • Account data: naam, email address (signup ke waqt).
  • Link data: aapke short kiye gaye URLs, unke titles, click counts aur banne ki date.
  • Earning data: wallet balance, withdrawal history aur payment details (jo aap withdraw ke waqt khud dete ho).

2. Data Kaise Use Hota Hai

  • Account chalane aur login dene ke liye.
  • Aapki kamai track karne aur payouts process karne ke liye.
  • Abuse/fraud detection ke liye (fake clicks waghera).

3. Cookies & Third-Party

Hum login session ke liye Firebase Auth use karte hain. Ad networks apni cookies laga sakti hain — unki apni policies hoti hain jinke liye hum zimmedar nahi hain.

4. Data Delete Karna

Aap Profile page se apna account delete kar sakte ho — uske saath saari links aur earning data bhi chala jayega.

5. Contact

Koi sawal ho to support email pe likho (footer/homepage contact section dekho).
