Pro Tips for Better Results

In tips se apne campaigns ko aur effective banao

Clear CTA Rakho

Har ad me ek clear Call-to-Action do - "Buy Now", "Learn More", "Get Offer" - user ko pata hona chahiye kya karna hai.

Eye-Catching Design

Bright colors use karo, high-quality images lagao, aur text ko chhota rakho. 3 second me user ka attention catch hona chahiye.

Mobile-First Design

70%+ traffic mobile se aata hai. Apne ad ko mobile pe test zaroor karo. Small screen pe sab kuch clear dikhna chahiye.

Right Timing

Festival, weekends, ya sale season pe ad chalao. Jab log free hote hain tab ads zyada dekhte hain.

Monitor & Optimize

Dashboard pe daily stats check karo. Agar CTR kam hai toh title, image ya target URL change karke test karo.

A/B Testing Karo

Same product ke liye 2 alag ads banao aur dekho kaunsa better perform kar raha hai. Data se decision lo!

Aksar Pooche Jaane Waale Sawal

Social Bar ads kya hain?
Social Bar ads ek sticky notification popup hain jo page ke bottom-right corner me dikhta hai. Ye non-intrusive hain aur users ko naturally attract karte hain bina page experience kharab kiye.
Video ads ka format kya hona chahiye?
MP4, WebM, ya GIF format me video de sakte ho. Recommended size: 720p (1280x720) aur duration 15-30 seconds. Chhota aur engaging video best perform karta hai.
Push notification ad kaise kaam karta hai?
Push notification ads phone ke notification style me dikhte hain. User ko ek alert aata hai jisme aapka title, description aur CTA hota hai. Ye sabse zyada open rate rakhta hai!
Minimum kitne impressions ka ad chal sakta hai?
Minimum 1,000 impressions ka package le sakte ho. Starter plan se shuru karo aur results dekho, fir upgrade karo!
Kitne time me ad approve hota hai?
Aamtaur par 24 ghante ke andar admin review karke approve kar deta hai. Kabhi-kabhi 2-3 ghante me bhi ho jata hai. Aapko dashboard pe status dikhega.
Payment kaunsa method hai?
Sirf paisa wallet se pay kar sakte ho. Pehle dashboard se wallet me add karo, fir campaign banao. Minimum wallet balance required hai package price ke barabar.
Refund mil sakta hai?
Agar ad approve nahi hua toh pura paisa wapas wallet me aa jayega. Agar ad already chal raha hai toh refund nahi hoga. Partially served campaigns me remaining impressions ka refund hota hai.
Ek saath kitne campaigns bana sakte hain?
Koi limit nahi hai! Jitne chahe utne campaigns bana sakte ho - social, video, push - sab ek saath chal sakte hain. Har campaign ka alag budget aur status hoga.
