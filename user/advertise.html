Minimum kitne impressions ka ad chalega?
Minimum 1,000 impressions ka package le sakte ho. Starter plan se shuru karo!
Kitne time me ad approve hota hai?
Aamtaur par 24 ghante ke andar admin approve kar deta hai. Kabhi-kabhi 2-3 ghante me bhi ho jata hai.
Kaunsi image size chahiye?
Banner ke liye 468x60, 728x90, 300x250, 160x600, 320x50 - koi bhi standard size chalega. PNG ya JPG dono accepted hain.
Payment kaunsa method hai?
Abhi sirf paisa wallet se pay kar sakte ho. Pehle wallet me add karo (dashboard se), fir campaign banao.
Refund mil sakta hai?
Agar ad approve nahi hua toh pura paisa wapas mil jayega. Agar chal raha hai toh refund nahi hoga.
