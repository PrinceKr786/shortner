Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about interstitial advertising on Shortify.

What are Interstitial Ads?
Interstitial ads are full-screen advertisements that cover the entire interface of the host app or site. They are displayed at natural transition points in the user flow. They offer maximum visibility and typically achieve 4-8x higher CTR compared to standard banner ads.
How does the countdown timer work?
When a visitor lands on your shortened link, the interstitial ad appears immediately. A countdown timer (5s, 10s, or 15s) runs and the user must wait for it to finish before they can skip or continue to the target URL. This ensures your ad gets genuine exposure time.
What CPM rate applies to interstitial ads?
Interstitial ads use the base banner CPM rate multiplied by 2, reflecting the premium nature and higher engagement of full-screen ads. The exact rate is loaded from our earning_rates system and is displayed when you select a package.
Can I change the countdown duration after creating?
No, once a campaign is created, the countdown duration cannot be changed. You will need to create a new campaign with the desired countdown setting. We recommend testing with 10 seconds first, as it provides the best balance of engagement and user experience.
How long does campaign approval take?
Most campaigns are reviewed and activated within 1-2 hours during business hours. Campaigns are checked for content compliance, image quality, and appropriate target URLs. You will see the status change from Pending to Active once approved.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP formats. For best results, use high-resolution images (1200x628px or similar aspect ratio). The image URL must be publicly accessible. Animated GIFs are supported but may increase load times.
Can I run multiple campaigns simultaneously?
Yes! You can run multiple interstitial ad campaigns at the same time. Each campaign is independent with its own budget, targeting, and countdown settings. This allows you to A/B test different creatives and offers.
