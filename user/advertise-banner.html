Banner Ad Best Practices

Keep It Clean

Use minimal text and bold visuals. Viewers have milliseconds to absorb your message. Let the image do the heavy lifting.

Strong Call-to-Action

Buttons like "Shop Now", "Learn More", or "Get Started" dramatically improve click-through rates.

Match Your Landing Page

Ensure your banner creative visually aligns with the landing page. Consistency builds trust and reduces bounce rates.

Use High-Quality Images

Blurry or pixelated banners hurt credibility. Use crisp, professionally designed graphics at the correct resolution.

Mobile First

Over 60% of traffic is mobile. Always include a 320×50 mobile banner in your campaign for maximum reach.

A/B Test Creatives

Run multiple banner variations simultaneously. After a few days, pause the low performers and double down on winners.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does campaign approval take?
Most banner campaigns are reviewed and approved within 2-4 hours during business hours. You'll receive a notification once approved.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP formats. Maximum file size is 500KB. We recommend JPG for photos and PNG for graphics with text.
Can I change my banner after submission?
Yes, you can edit your campaign while it's still in "pending" status. Once approved, contact support to request changes.
How are impressions counted?
An impression is counted each time your banner is fully loaded and visible on a page for at least 1 second. We use server-side tracking for accuracy.
What is the minimum budget?
Our smallest package is 1,000 impressions. You can run multiple campaigns simultaneously to spread your budget across different creatives.
Can I pause a running campaign?
Yes, you can pause any active campaign from the My Campaigns section. Remaining impressions will be saved and you can resume anytime.
